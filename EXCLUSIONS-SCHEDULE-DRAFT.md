This schedule must not be activated until a qualified lawyer has reviewed the proposed criteria under the governing law, the law where access is offered, applicable anti-discrimination law, sanctions law, and the hosting platform's terms.

## 1. Baseline organisational exclusion

The core licence already excludes every Organisation and every natural person acting for, at the direction of, under the control of, or for the material benefit of an Organisation. This schedule should not duplicate that rule.

Do not define an excluded class by race, colour, descent, ethnicity, national origin, birthplace, religion, disability, sex, or another protected or inherent characteristic without specific written legal advice that the rule is lawful, necessary, and proportionate.

## 3. Excluded territories

No territory is excluded by this draft.

For any proposed entry, counsel must specify which connecting fact controls. These are not interchangeable:

- physical location when access occurs;
- ordinary residence;
- intended location of inspection or use;
- citizenship or nationality;
- location of an associated organisation, server, or beneficiary; or
- designation under a named sanctions regime.

## 5. Protective process

Before adding an entry, retain a confidential decision record stating:

1. the concrete risk the exclusion addresses;
2. why a less discriminatory actor-, conduct-, purpose-, or access-based control is inadequate;
3. the evidence and its reliability;
4. the rule's necessity, proportionality, scope, and duration;
5. effects on innocent people, humanitarian work, and vulnerable groups;
6. applicable legal and platform constraints;
7. review, correction, exception, and appeal procedures; and
8. the approving lawyer and decision-maker.

Every active entry should expire unless affirmatively renewed. Emergency suspension can be shorter and should receive prompt independent review.
