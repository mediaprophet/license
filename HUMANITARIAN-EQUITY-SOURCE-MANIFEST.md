## Source and authority discipline

New drafts should preserve the source passage, its context, whether it is human direction or an agent proposal where discernible, the adopted interpretation, reviewer, version and unresolved questions. Do not infer authorship from prose style. When the source does not establish who made a statement, record that uncertainty. A human correction can establish project intent without proving a proposition of law; an agent proposal can contain a useful idea without being an authoritative source.

The source's placeholder identities, informal choices of “international law”, generated citations, ontology mappings and provider-term assertions are not verified legal foundations. Each proposed operative clause needs relevant primary authority, jurisdiction and legal-effect review. Existing [humanitarian](HUMANITARIAN-FOUNDATIONS.md), [OECD](OECD-SOURCE-MANIFEST.md) and [Web Civics](WEB-CIVICS-SOURCE-MANIFEST.md) sources remain distinct from this design-lineage record.

The development concern described as digital slavery connects exploitation of labour and resources with loss of human agency, coercion and informational control. It should not collapse different harms into an unsupported legal classification. Review the concrete conduct, affected humans, authority, duties and available remedies.

## Boundary of this reconciliation

This update adds design specifications, worked cases and a staged implementation handoff. It does not activate the historical licence, import its ontologies, implement its enumerator or assert that the new records are accepted by the current generator schema. QualiaDB source versions and candidate integration points are recorded separately in the [implementation roadmap](QUALIADB-IMPLEMENTATION-ROADMAP.md). The next phase must validate both syntax and meaning against the selected tools before relying on their results.
