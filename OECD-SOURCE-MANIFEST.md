## Source-use rules

1. Cite the official Compendium identifier for a legal instrument and record the version consulted.
2. Distinguish Recommendations, Declarations, reports, explanatory material, domestic law, treaty obligations, and contract terms.
3. Do not present an OECD policy paper as legislation or an OECD recommendation as a self-executing private contract.
4. Record jurisdiction, adherence, implementation, amendments, reservations where relevant, and review date before adopting an operative clause.
5. Preserve the distinction between quoted source text, faithful summary, project policy, legal interpretation, and machine-proposed mapping.
